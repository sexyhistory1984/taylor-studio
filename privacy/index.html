Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026

This page covers taylor.famemaker.app and FameMaker, the studio's own tool for posting its designs to its social media accounts. Questions: tarthwell@gmail.com.

This website

  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking cookies. The site does not set cookies.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security (see GitHub's privacy statement). Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when fonts download.
  • Stored on your device only. The shop remembers whether you prefer the card or grid view and which designs you ticked in a "build the set" checklist. This stays in your browser's local storage and is never sent to us.
  • Buying. Every purchase happens on Redbubble, which prints and ships the order. Redbubble's own privacy policy covers your order, payment and delivery details; we never see your card details. Links to Redbubble and links shared on social media carry tags showing which page or network the visit came from, not who you are.

FameMaker, the studio's posting tool

  • FameMaker runs on the studio owner's own computer. It posts the studio's own product images and descriptions to the studio's own accounts on networks such as Pinterest, Bluesky, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, X, LinkedIn and Tumblr, using only the permissions the owner grants when connecting each account.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on that computer. They are not sent to us or anyone else.
  • FameMaker does not read, collect or store information about other people: not followers, not messages, not contacts. It reads only the connected account's own name, handle and, for Pinterest, its own boards.
  • Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Deleting data

  • Website visitors: we hold no personal data about you. To clear the shop preferences, clear this site's data in your browser.
  • Connected accounts: disconnecting an account in FameMaker (Marketing → Social → Disconnect) deletes its token from the Keychain. You can also remove the app's access in the network's own settings (for example Facebook → Settings → Apps and Websites), which revokes it immediately.
  • For anything else, email tarthwell@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

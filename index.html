FAQ
Questions people ask before ordering.

    

      
How fast is delivery, really?
Most Basic tiers deliver in one to two days and Premium tiers in three to four. Rush delivery compresses that to 24 hours for a 50% premium. Your written scope states the exact date.

      
Which sources do you use for research?
Public websites, published pricing pages, filings, reviews and reports. We do not access paid databases or private data, and every item in a brief links to where it came from.

      
Can you work from rough notes?
Yes, that is the normal starting point. Bullet points, a voice memo transcript or a messy spreadsheet is enough to scope the job.

      
Is a policy or SOP you draft legal advice?
No. We produce business drafts. Any policy with legal effect should be reviewed by a qualified professional in your jurisdiction.

      
Do I own the deliverable?
Yes. Once the order is complete, the document, spreadsheet, script or image and any template are yours to use and edit.

      
How do payments work?
Orders placed on Fiverr are protected by Fiverr's escrow and are released on delivery. Direct projects are quoted in writing first and invoiced through PayPal.

      
Who does the work?
I scope and review every project personally. Our team handles gathering, drafting and checking under a documented quality process, which is how we keep prices low and turnaround short.

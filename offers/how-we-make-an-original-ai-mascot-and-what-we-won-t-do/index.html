How we make an original AI mascot (and what we won't do)

See packages and order

A mascot gives a small brand a face. Here is our process.

1. Brief. You tell us your brand, audience, colors and the mood you want.

2. Concept and character sheet. We write a character concept, a palette and pose notes with AI assistance.

3. Generation. The owner generates the images by hand with an AI image tool. Nothing is scripted or automated.

4. Delivery. You get transparent PNG poses, the character sheet and a written commercial-use grant.

What we won't do: use any real person's likeness, copy existing characters or brands, or hide that the images are AI-generated. Every delivery is labeled as AI-generated.

Packages start at $25 for 2 poses. Standard is $45 for 4 poses and Premium is $85 for 8 poses plus a social kit. Delivery takes 2-3 days. The work is done by the TradeUp owner and team.

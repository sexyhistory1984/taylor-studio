Before you import contacts into a CRM, a quick cleanup can prevent duplicate records and messy segments. Here is the checklist we work from:

1. Pick a duplicate rule. Decide what counts as a duplicate: the same email, or the same name plus company. Log every merge so you can undo it.

2. Lowercase emails and check their syntax. Catch typos such as missing @ signs. Note that a syntax check does not confirm the mailbox exists.

3. Standardise phone numbers to E.164. Set a default country for numbers that have no country code.

4. Normalise names, company names and countries. Use consistent capitalisation and one spelling per country.

5. Keep a change log. It lets you trace what changed and why.

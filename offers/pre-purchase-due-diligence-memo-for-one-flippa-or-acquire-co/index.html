Service

Pre-purchase due-diligence memo for one Flippa or Acquire.com listing

Send your listing URL and order a memo

Before you buy a website, SaaS or newsletter: get an independent written memo

Who it is for

First-time buyers looking at a micro-SaaS, content site or newsletter listed on Flippa or Acquire.com who want a second set of eyes on the seller's claims before sending money.

What you receive

A written memo on one listing that covers:

  • Revenue-claim consistency checks (traffic vs revenue, screenshot quality, gaps and spikes)
  • Traffic, platform and transfer red flags
  • 10-15 specific questions to ask the seller
  • A payback calculation in base, downside and claimed cases
  • A clear verdict: proceed, proceed with conditions, or walk away

How it works

1. Order and send the listing URL plus any seller documents you are permitted to share.

2. The owner and team, using AI-assisted research, review the listing and documents.

3. You receive the memo within 48 hours.

Price

Launch price: $40 per listing memo. This is a new service and the price is an introductory launch price. Deals above $50k: please message before ordering.

What happens after you order

You answer a short intake (listing URL, asking price, your budget and goals, documents shared). The memo is delivered within 48 hours through the platform. You can ask follow-up questions about the memo.

Important: this is research only, not legal, tax or financial advice. We do not contact sellers on your behalf, and the memo relies on the information available in the listing and documents you provide. Work is delivered by the owner and team with AI assistance.

Why secondary listing images matter, and a $25 way to fill them

See the offer and order

Most marketplaces let you add several images to each listing. Many small sellers fill the first slot with a plain product shot and leave the rest empty. Lifestyle scenes, which show the product in a setting like a kitchen, desk or shelf, can help shoppers picture how the item is used.

We now offer a simple service. You send one clear product photo, and we deliver 5 AI-generated lifestyle scenes within 48 h, with 1 revision, for $25.

How we keep it honest:

  • Clear labeling. Every scene is AI-generated and labeled that way.
  • No people. We use no people and no real-person likenesses.
  • Your rights. You confirm you own the rights to your photo.
  • Marketplace rules. Check your marketplace's image rules before you upload.

The work is done by the TradeUp owner and team using a manual AI image workflow. This is a new service, so we have no client results to share yet.

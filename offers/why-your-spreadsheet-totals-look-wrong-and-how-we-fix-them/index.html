If your totals or counts look wrong, the cause is often something small:

  • duplicate rows;
  • dates stored as text;
  • numbers with stray spaces;
  • the same customer spelled three ways.

These common issues can break SUMs, filters and pivots without any warning.

Our cleanup service handles them in a set order:

1. Find duplicates using the column you choose.

2. Convert dates and numbers into real values.

3. Standardise names and text case.

4. Flag anything missing or suspicious instead of deleting it.

You get a cleaned copy, and a change log so you can see every edit. Your original file stays untouched.

Want to try it yourself first? Two quick checks:

  • Dates and numbers: select a column and look at the alignment. Text usually aligns left and numbers right, unless formatting has been changed.
  • Name variants: sort your name column and scan for near-duplicates.

Moss the Bog Frog

A sleepy little bog frog in a mushroom cap who loves his ferns. Moss is an original character, and the artwork was created with AI assistance and edited by the artist.

Who it is for

Plant parents, cottagecore fans and frog lovers who want a small, cheerful sticker for a laptop, water bottle or planner.

What you receive

A printed Moss the Bog Frog sticker (other products may be offered where available). It is printed and shipped by the print-on-demand marketplace (Redbubble) when you order.

How it works

1. Click through to the Moss listing on Redbubble.

2. Choose your product and size.

3. Check out on Redbubble. Redbubble handles payment, printing and delivery.

Price

The price is shown on the Redbubble listing at checkout. It is set by the platform's base price plus a small artist markup.

What happens after you order

Redbubble prints your item and ships it to you. Their order tracking, shipping times and returns policy apply. Moss is designed and listed by a small independent team. The owner and team handle the design and the listing, and Redbubble handles fulfillment.

Disclosure: Artwork created with AI assistance and edited by the artist. Moss is an original character and does not depict any real person or existing brand character.

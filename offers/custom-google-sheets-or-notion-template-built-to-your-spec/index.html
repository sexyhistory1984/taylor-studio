Custom Google Sheets or Notion Template Built to Your Spec

Order your custom template

A Google Sheets or Notion template built around how you work

Who it is for

Small business owners, freelancers and solo operators who need a tracker (budget, client CRM, content calendar, inventory, project list) and don't want to adapt a generic template or build one from scratch.

What you receive

  • A clean, tested Google Sheets or Notion template built to your spec
  • Dropdowns, formulas and automatic totals where they help
  • A short how-to note explaining how to use and edit it
  • Delivered as a copyable link you own

How it works

1. Choose a package and order.

2. Answer a few short questions: what you track, which columns or fields you need, and any example you like.

3. We draft the template with AI assistance, then build and test it by hand.

4. You receive a copyable link and how-to note, with target delivery within 2 days of receiving your answers.

Price

  • Basic: $15, 1-tab Google Sheets tracker
  • Standard: $35, up to 4 tabs plus a summary dashboard
  • Premium: $60, Notion workspace plus a short guide

What happens after you order

You'll get the requirements questions right away. Once answered, work starts and you receive your template link within the stated delivery time. If something doesn't match your brief, tell us and we'll adjust it within the package scope.

Work is delivered by the TradeUp owner and team. Drafting is AI-assisted; every template is checked and tested by a person before delivery. This is a new offer, so we have no reviews to show yet; sample templates are available on request.

Why Moss wears a mushroom hat

Meet Moss

Moss is a bog frog who naps under ferns, and his mushroom cap keeps the drizzle off. We designed him as a small, original companion for anyone whose windowsill is more jungle than sill. He started as a character spec that we wrote, and the artwork was created with AI assistance and edited by the artist. Moss is available as a sticker through Redbubble, which prints and ships each order. If your planner needs a frog, Moss is ready.

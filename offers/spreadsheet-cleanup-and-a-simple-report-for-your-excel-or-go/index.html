Service

Spreadsheet cleanup and a simple report for your Excel or Google Sheets data

Order the $20 Basic cleanup on Fiverr

Clean, usable spreadsheet data and a simple report

Delivered by the owner and team, with AI assistance. Every change is checked by a person before delivery.

Who it is for

  • Small business owners and solo founders with messy customer, sales, inventory or contact lists.
  • Anyone whose sheet has duplicates, mixed date formats, inconsistent names or numbers stored as text.
  • People who want a quick summary of what their data says without building pivots themselves.

What you receive

  • Your cleaned file (Excel or Google Sheets). Your original data is never overwritten. We work on a copy.
  • A change log listing what was changed and why.
  • Flags, not deletions. Missing or suspicious values are highlighted for your decision. They are never silently removed.
  • Standard and Premium: pivot summaries, charts and a short findings report.

How it works

1. Order a package on Fiverr and share your file plus a short note on what "clean" means for you. For example, which column identifies a duplicate.

2. We remove duplicates. We fix dates, numbers and text case, and standardise names.

3. We review every change, then build your summary if your package includes one.

4. You receive the cleaned file, the change log and any report. Questions are answered through Fiverr messages.

Price

| Package | Scope | Delivery | Price |

|---|---|---|---|

| Basic | 1 sheet, up to 1,000 rows | 1 day | $20 |

| Standard | up to 3 sheets / 5,000 rows + summary | 2 days | $50 |

| Premium | up to 5 sheets / 10,000 rows + pivots, charts, report | 3 days | $100 |

These are introductory prices for a new service. Payment is handled by Fiverr checkout.

What happens after you order

  • We confirm the scope within the delivery window. If your file is larger than the package covers, we tell you before starting.
  • Please remove passwords and any data you do not want shared. Your file is used only to deliver your order.
  • You review the delivery. If a change does not match your instructions, request a revision through Fiverr.

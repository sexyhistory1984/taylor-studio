5 AI Lifestyle Scenes From Your Product Photo: $25, 48 h Delivery

Order 5 scenes for $25

5 AI Lifestyle Scenes From One Product Photo

Who it is for

Small sellers on Etsy, Shopify or Amazon who have a clean product photo and want extra lifestyle or background images for their secondary listing slots. You need no photoshoot and no props.

What you receive

  • 5 AI-generated lifestyle scenes built around your product photo
  • High-resolution image files
  • 1 revision round
  • Delivery within 48 hours of receiving your photo and brief

All scenes are AI-generated, and we disclose that clearly. No people or real-person likenesses appear in any scene.

How it works

1. Order and send one clear product photo, ideally on a plain background.

2. Tell us the setting you want, for example a kitchen counter, a desk or an outdoor table, plus any style notes.

3. We produce the scenes. The owner generates them manually with an AI image tool, and our small team reviews each one.

4. You review the 5 scenes and can request 1 revision.

Price

$25 for 5 scenes, delivered in 48 h, with 1 revision included.

What happens after you order

  • You confirm that you own the rights to the photo you send.
  • We check that the photo is usable and ask follow-up questions if needed.
  • You receive 5 high-res files within 48 h.
  • If you want changes, you request your 1 revision and we deliver the updated files.

Please review your marketplace's rules on AI-generated or edited images before you upload the scenes. Work is delivered by the TradeUp owner and team. This is a new service and we have no past client results to show yet.

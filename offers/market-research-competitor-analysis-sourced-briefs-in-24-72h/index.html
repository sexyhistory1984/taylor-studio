Market Research & Competitor Analysis: Sourced Briefs in 24-72h

Who it is for

Small business owners, founders and managers who need a clear answer to a specific question (who are my competitors, which vendor fits, what does this market look like) and do not need a 40-page report.

What you receive

  • A concise brief, competitor table or vendor comparison focused on your question
  • A link to a checkable source for every factual claim
  • Gaps flagged clearly instead of guessed
  • One revision included

We also handle data cleanup and document drafting.

How it works

1. Send your question, your deadline and any context, such as competitors you already know or vendors you are considering.

2. You receive a fixed quote before any work starts.

3. The research is AI-assisted. A person verifies and edits it before delivery.

4. You receive the deliverable within 24-72h, depending on scope.

Price

Starter brief: $75 fixed. Larger scopes are quoted as a fixed price up front. Payment is made through platform checkout (Upwork).

What happens after you order

We confirm the scope and sources we plan to check, then deliver within the agreed window. You can request one revision. If a question cannot be answered from public sources, we tell you rather than fill the gap with guesses.

The work is delivered by the owner and a small team using AI-assisted research tools. All output is verified and edited before delivery.

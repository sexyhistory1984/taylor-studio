Service

Original AI-Generated Mascot Character for Your Brand

Order your mascot

A mascot made for your shop, podcast, stream or game. The character is original, the images are AI-generated and disclosed as such, and you get a written commercial-use grant.

Who it is for

  • Small businesses and Etsy shops that want a recognizable character
  • Indie game developers who need a mascot or placeholder hero
  • Podcasters and streamers who want a channel character

What you receive

  • 1 original character. It is not based on any real person's likeness and does not copy any existing character.
  • Poses as transparent PNG files. The number of poses depends on your package.
  • A color palette and a character sheet
  • A written commercial-use grant for the delivered images
  • An AI-generated disclosure note you can keep with your files

How it works

1. You order and answer a short brief: your brand, audience, vibe, colors, and anything to avoid.

2. Our team writes the character concept and sheet with AI assistance.

3. The owner generates the images by hand with an AI image tool. No real-person likeness and no copied characters are used.

4. You receive the files and the license.

Price

  • Basic: $25, 2 poses
  • Standard: $45, 4 poses
  • Premium: $85, 8 poses plus a social kit

Delivery takes 2-3 days.

What happens after you order

You receive a brief questionnaire. We confirm the concept with you before generating images. Files are delivered through the order platform with the commercial-use grant attached. All work is delivered by the TradeUp owner and team. Please note that AI-generated images may have limited copyright protection in some jurisdictions. The grant covers our rights in the delivered files.

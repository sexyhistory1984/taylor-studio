Five checks to run on a small online business listing before you buy

Order a memo for your listing

Buying a micro-SaaS, content site or newsletter usually means relying on numbers the seller reports. Here are checks we run in every memo:

1. Does traffic plausibly support the stated revenue? Compare the traffic source and volume with the monetization method.

2. Look at the revenue screenshots: are dates, currencies and totals consistent? Are there sudden spikes or gaps?

3. Platform dependence: how much depends on one search engine, marketplace or ad network?

4. Transfer risk: domains, accounts, payment processors and integrations that may not transfer cleanly.

5. Payback in three cases: claimed numbers, a base case and a downside case.

If you want these checks done for a specific listing, we write a fixed-price memo with 10-15 seller questions and a clear verdict, delivered in 48 h for a $40 launch price. Prepared by the owner and team with AI assistance; research only, not legal, tax or financial advice.
